# Grant Sacco: personal site

## Direction (most important)

This is **not a resume on a website**. It should feel like:

> This is Grant. Here are the things he's built, the places he's been, the problems he's worked on, the photos he's taken, and the weird side projects he makes because he's curious.

- **Show, don't tell.** The five themes (builder, field-oriented engineer, problem solver, internationally curious, active/adventurous) are proven by the projects, photography, travel, languages, sports, startup and apps. Don't add more personality words or adjectives to the site.
- **Not a resume.** No PMP, no HOA board, no skill lists as content. Personal context (family, sports, travel) supports the work quietly and never crowds it.
- **Uncluttered.** Each section shows a few hand-picked artifacts; everything else lives in a "+ more" list.
- **One quiet line, at most.** About carries "Based in Austin · Mechanical engineer, BSME · 8+ years in manufacturing · English, Italian" (a few key facts, wrapping between items) and nothing more like it. Jaylee and Helga (the family dogs) sit on the 2021 ring in About as one small sprite each (named on hover), never as a tile.
- **Keep personal tags off the work.** No languages on the Travel map; no sports on films or trips.

## Where things are

- `planning/site-artifacts.xlsx`: the master list of artifacts and decisions (sections, crossovers, featured, open questions, places, films, profile). Grant edits it; keep it in sync when he answers questions.
- `src/lens/artifacts.js`: Lens's real content (roles, engineering, projects, apps, films, places lived), taken from the workbook. Pictures are placeholders until real photos, CAD and the logo arrive; `tbd` marks placeholder dates. A real image goes in as `picture: { src, bg }` (cover-cropped to 3:2 and re-rendered in every lens): KU, the racecar (Grant's render), the palletizer (Grant's render on white), the static mixer (its CAD render), OnlyChargeEV (an install photo, licence plates blurred) and the ProMade work center (the EBM room) have real ones, the last two trimmed and framed 3:2 on their own background so nothing is cropped; an open project page repaints when its picture arrives.
- `src/content.js`: sample content for mocks 1–6 (Lens only takes the section names from it).
- **Hosting and analytics:** Vercel builds the repo (`npm run build` → `dist`) and serves it at grantsacco-4v69.vercel.app (the canonical URL in `index.html`: link previews, canonical and the Person schema use it, so change them together if a custom domain is added). Vercel Web Analytics is injected in `src/lens2/main.js` (`@vercel/analytics`, production builds only), so it needs Web Analytics enabled in the Vercel project.
- **List view** (`/work/`, `work/index.html`): every project as a plain, static list for a quick scan and for search engines (Experience, Engineering, Projects, Digital, Art & photography, films with album links, places), each linking back to its page in the interactive site (`../?p=id#section`). It is filled in from `src/lens/artifacts.js` and `src/content.js` by the `work-list` plugin in `vite.config.js` (renderer: `scripts/work-list.js`), so it never needs editing by hand. The header links to it ("List view" on desktop, a small ≡ button on phones, hidden below 340px), and so does the About card. `public/robots.txt` and `public/sitemap.xml` list both pages.
- **Brand pieces** (in `public/`): the icon is the About wheel's origin, white rings and a dot on the site's blue (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192/512.png`, `site.webmanifest`); `og.jpg` (1200 × 630) is the link-preview card: headshot, "Advanced manufacturing engineering · Austin, TX", name, role, a line on the work, four equal prints in one blue duotone with captions (Automated dry mix plant: the tower photo; Additive manufacturing: the EBM room; Clean pack & sterilization: the MPOC model's hidden-line view with the suite lit; Formula SAE electric) and the URL.
- **The site is Lens (v2), standalone at the root** (`index.html` → `src/lens2/main.js`): no concept gallery, no links to other versions, a header with only the name and Get in touch (`mountChrome(..., { standalone: true })`). The build contains only it. The concept mocks (`/scale`, `/viewport`, `/coordinates`, `/layers`, `/translation`, `/morph`, `/lens`, `/lens2`) stay in the repo for reference and still run in `npm run dev`, but are not built or linked.
- `/lens2` (Lens v2, `src/lens2/`) is a copy of Lens with a quieter, more professional look: one palette (ink, paper, one calm blue; Art and Digital dark), one sans and one mono, hairlines instead of shadows and glows, plain prints instead of polaroids, technical labels instead of handwriting, restrained picture renders (colour kept). It shares Lens's content (`src/lens2/artifacts.js` re-exports `src/lens/artifacts.js`), so content changes go in one place. v2 was chosen: behaviour changes go in `src/lens2/` only (v1 is frozen). Its section renders keep the real picture under every effect (Engineering: the photo washed and inked; Projects: clay with a trace of colour; Digital: the dimmed photo under the characters) so every object stays recognisable.

## Lens decisions so far

- Company names and logos: project first, company second. Logos are small and sparing: company logos (Enovis, MAPEI, OnlyChargeEV, in `src/lens/img/logos/`) sit under the work in About only (KU's tile already is the school's mark, so it carries no logo underneath; the small KU logo stays in the folder for use elsewhere); an app's icon (ContactFlow) sits beside its name in Digital only. No big branded tiles. Check what can be shown publicly about Enovis before launch.

- Sections keep their coordinate systems; a render line re-renders everything when switching. The sixth section is called **Digital** (was Apps): apps, code, electronics and controls.
- About is a calendar wheel: rings are years, the angle is the time of year (January at the top, clockwise), each piece at its start month. Only career, school and a few key projects live there (the Enovis ones: the operations facility, ProMade and clean pack & sterilization, the last at April 2026 as a placeholder; MAPEI Corp sits at August 2021, also a placeholder, which keeps it clear of KU on short laptop screens; no trips, no AWC; MAPEI Corp's card carries both stints, Chicago 2018–19 and Dallas 2021–24). The time of year is cued simply ("Jan" at the top and a "through the year" arrow), with no month names. Races are their logos, small, on the wheel at their year and month, named on hover (Austin Marathon, full 26.2, Feb 2024; IRONMAN 70.3 Texas, spring 2026).
- About's pictures read as one set of prints: one shared grade (`reality` in `src/lens2/render.js`), a thin white mat with a hairline on every tile, and every role badge drawn the same way (paper, a faint grid, the name in ink, place and years in mono, one short blue rule).
- Clicking the origin ("Grant" at the centre of About, which pulses softly and carries a small blue "About me ↗" chip; tiles keep clear of both) opens a small card; the headshot also sits small and round beside the name in the header and opens the same card from any section. The card holds: headshot (`src/lens/img/headshot.jpg`, Grant in Rio, cropped to the face), three short paragraphs in Grant's own voice (first person), mixed from his résumé summary and his notes (mechanical engineer and project manager, 8+ years delivering advanced manufacturing and industrial facilities, owning projects end to end; medical technology at Enovis, leading the build-out of a facility for end-to-end orthopedic implant manufacturing, through cleaning, packaging and sterilization to shipping; chemical manufacturing plants and lines for MAPEI, and OnlyChargeEV), then one personal paragraph under a hairline (Chicago, Milan and Italian, travel and languages, running, surfing, outdoors; `intro` and `intro.personal` in `src/lens/artifacts.js`), a small blue "Mechanical engineer · Austin" under the name, and email (gsacco15@gmail.com, which Grant asked to show), LinkedIn and the résumé PDF (`public/grant-sacco-resume.pdf`); the Get in touch card has the same (all from `site` in `src/content.js`). Room names, numbers, metadata and drawing references are dropped; the Pascal file itself never goes in the repo. It opens from the dock side, like his model; the plan puts the offices at the top. - The second is MAPEI Houston: one facility that holds the automated dry mix plant and the liquid admixtures plant (and is the 315K sq ft site development). Grant's own exterior model (`src/lens/img/models/mapei-drymix.glb`, v12, loaded with GLTFLoader) is split in `model3d.js` into four zones: Silo & mixing tower; Production & warehouse (the packaging lines and the admixtures plant are inside); Offices; Site (highlights from the legend only). All three projects use it: the dry mix plant opens with the tower lit, the admixtures expansion with the plant building lit, the site development with nothing lit. It is outside only, so its roof stays on except in the exploded build. Each model is a definition in `model3d.js` (zones, cameras, looks, build); a new building is a new definition.
- The third is the KU assistive piano-pedal device (`src/lens/img/models/piano-pedal.glb`): a visual reconstruction from photos (not CAD), simplified from Grant's 4 MB file, shown 250× so it sits at the buildings' scale. Its parts group by name into six zones (T-grip handle; Actuating shaft; Pivot & linkage; Inclined pedal plate; Spring return; Base plate); the Projects tab pulls them apart into an exploded assembly (`explode` per zone) and the twin shows the push down the shaft into the pedal.
- Model tiles are stills rendered from the model itself (iso view, site left out; the admixtures expansion with its zone lit, the site development from higher up with its site), and in About they turn on hover: 72 frames per model in one sprite (`*-turn.jpg`), drawn over the still by `turntable.js` (both Lens copies), slowly spinning and following the pointer, settling back on the still when it leaves. No WebGL in the field. The dry mix plant sits in About for this (2022 ring, month a placeholder). Stills and sprites are re-rendered from the model whenever it changes.
